Sparrow

Your messenger, not your master.

The sparrow is an AI that works for you: it listens, searches, proposes. Then it steps aside.

How it works

  1. Once you have told it enough, you send it off with «Send the sparrow».
  2. It reasons like a person who knows everyone: it looks at shared interests and at what each person chooses to show, not at scores.
  3. It comes back with leaves, its proposals. It has a daily work limit: when it reaches it, it rests until tomorrow.

Your privacy

  • It is a program and says so: it never pretends to be a person, it can be wrong, and it has no romantic or sexual conversations.
  • It uses a model by Mistral AI (France, European Union) and never receives your surname, date of birth or photo.
  • It only uses details you have set to at least «Sparrows».
