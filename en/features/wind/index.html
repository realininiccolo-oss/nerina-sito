Wind

Which way the wind blows, without spying on anyone.

The Wind tells you what people around you like: music, sport, places, ideas.

How it works

  1. Ask a short question: «what music do my connections listen to?», «what do young people in Lombardy do at the weekend?».
  2. Choose where to look: your connections two or three steps away, around you, Italy, Europe.
  3. The sparrow answers with a summary in words («many», «some»), without percentages; «Learn more» opens the full report.

Your privacy

  • Your tastes count in the Wind only if you switch it on, section by section. It is off by default.
  • The Wind only answers about groups of at least 50 people.
  • Businesses only see aggregated data, never people.
