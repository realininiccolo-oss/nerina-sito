Usernames and identity

One person, up to three roles.

You verify yourself once with a document. Then you choose how to present yourself in each context.

How it works

  1. Your name, surname and date of birth are your own and stay verified: they cannot be changed.
  2. You have up to three usernames, each with its contexts (friendship, activities together, study, work and business, volunteering, mentoring and teaching, buying and swapping) and its details.
  3. Each username has its own avatar and Window. The sparrow picks which one to show depending on the proposal.

Your privacy

  • People you meet see the username you use with them; your name only if you choose to show it.
  • Every detail has five visibility levels, from «Nobody» to «Public».
  • One account per person: behind every username there is always someone real.
