Diary

Tell it, in writing or out loud.

The Diary is where you talk to the sparrow. It is not a wall: nobody else reads what you write.

How it works

  1. Write or speak what you need, or how your day went. The sparrow asks questions until it understands.
  2. When it is ready, it offers to fly: tap «Send the sparrow» and it goes searching.
  3. In «Memories» you tell it how you want to be listened to; in «Topics» you see and correct what it has understood about you.

Your privacy

  • The day's text stays only in memory until «Close the day»: it is not written to disk.
  • Audio becomes text and is not stored.
  • Sensitive details (for example religion or orientation) are saved only with your yes, one by one.
