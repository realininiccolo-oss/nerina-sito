Events

From dinner to the tournament.

Events bring several people together: a dinner, an exhibition, a card tournament at the parish hall.

How it works

  1. Three views: Invitations, Going, Organising. You search public events with a sentence, no filters.
  2. Organisers choose the invitation: public, or targeted, with the sparrow taking the leaf to people who match the description, up to a maximum number.
  3. The sparrow suggests events from what you tell the Diary.

Your privacy

  • You choose who sees that you are going: by default, nobody.
  • Whoever organises an event is registered and responsible for it.
