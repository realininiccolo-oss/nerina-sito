Network

Your people, in order.

The Network gathers your connections: as a list, in areas, or as a map of the people you are linked to.

How it works

  1. You connect with the other person's username and Window code, without giving your name.
  2. You group connections into areas with a name and a colour, and give everyone a name of your own.
  3. The sparrow is keener to propose meetings with the connections you like.

Your privacy

  • With «Exclude» you hide what you share from your connections, or from theirs too.
  • Each username has its own Window: 16 digits you give only to whom you want.
  • A block applies to the person, across all their usernames.
