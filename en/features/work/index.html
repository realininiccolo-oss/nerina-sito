Work

You decide who sees your CV.

Job ads and applications go through leaves, with clear rules for both sides.

How it works

  1. You fill in your CV and choose, part by part, who can see it.
  2. Businesses publish the pay, and you see exactly the experience they ask for.
  3. The sparrow brings you suitable ads as leaves; you can state the salary you would like.

Your privacy

  • The business never sees your ideal salary as a number.
  • No solely automated decisions: a person at the business always decides.
  • People aged 16 or 17 only see ads open to their age.
