Leaves

Proposals, not a feed.

A leaf is a proposal from the sparrow: a person, a group for an activity, a place, an event or a job.

How it works

  1. In a leaf you see the other person's username and the things you share that they choose to show.
  2. You accept or decline. The sparrow only proposes when the meeting makes sense for both of you.
  3. If you both accept, you can write in the short chat and meet: between strangers, in a public place.

Your privacy

  • For friendship and opportunities, the kind of connection is revealed only when everyone accepts.
  • The short chat has few messages, is deleted after 24 hours and does not allow images.
  • Anyone you block disappears from your leaves, with all their usernames.
