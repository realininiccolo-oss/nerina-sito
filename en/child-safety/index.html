Child safety on Nerina

Standards against child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE)

This page applies to Nerina, the app by Nerina Devs. On Google Play it is listed as "Nerina: people, work, places". Nerina is published under the name Nerina Devs. The data controller is named in the privacy policy. See Contact. It says what is forbidden, how we protect people aged 16 or 17, how to report and what we do next.

What is forbidden

Child sexual abuse and exploitation are never allowed on Nerina. A child is anyone under 18.

You must not:

  • post, send, ask for or exchange child sexual abuse material (CSAM): photos, videos, text, drawings, including material made with AI;
  • say where to find it or how to get it;
  • try to gain a child's trust for sexual purposes (grooming);
  • talk about a child in a sexual way, or describe a child that way;
  • ask a child for intimate photos, or threaten a child to get them;
  • push a child to move to another app or to meet in secret for sexual purposes;
  • offer or look for children for exploitation or trafficking.

This applies to profiles, photos, messages, connection requests, events and what you write to the sparrow. Anyone who does this loses their account and is reported to the authorities.

How Nerina protects you

  • Verified age. You can join from the age of 16, after your ID card is verified. People under 16 cannot join.
  • Nobody sees you before verification. A pending account receives no proposals and no requests.
  • If you are 16 or 17:
    • we only suggest people within 3 years of your age;
    • people over 20 cannot reach you with invites or targeted proposals;
    • nobody can send you a photo in a connection request, and you cannot send one;
    • people over 20 can only write you a short text message, which you can report;
    • your profile photo is visible to your connections at most.
  • Looking for a partner (dating). In the App Store app (iPhone) it does not exist for anyone. In the Google Play app it exists only from the age of 18: it is not available to people aged 16 or 17. Where looking for a partner is available, people aged 16 or 17 are only matched with people at most 3 years apart. Your age group is computed on our server from your verified date of birth.
  • Short chat. Text only, no images. Messages disappear after 24 hours.
  • The sparrow. It is an AI. It does not hold romantic or sexual conversations, with anyone.
  • Meeting. Proposals name a public place.
  • Block. You can block anyone. The block covers all of that person's usernames.

How to report

Inside the app, without leaving it:

  • tap Report on a profile, a photo, a message, a connection request, an event or an answer from the sparrow;
  • or write to support@nerina.eu: the address is also in the app, under Profile.

Outside the app, even without an account: write to support@nerina.eu.

Reports that involve a child come first. We aim to decide them within 24 hours. We tell you the outcome.

If someone is in danger now, call 112 (in Italy and the EU). In Italy, for an emergency involving a child you can also call 114 Emergenza Infanzia. If you are under 18 in Italy and want to talk to someone, Telefono Azzurro answers on 19696.

What we do

When we learn of child sexual abuse material (CSAM) or of an attempt at grooming:

  1. we remove the content at once and block access to it;
  2. we stop the account of the person who posted it, then close it;
  3. we report it to the Polizia Postale, the Italian police unit for these crimes, and we cooperate with the authorities;
  4. we keep the reported material only for as long as the law requires for the authorities [?]. We show it to nobody else;
  5. we protect the person who reported: the reported person is not told who it was.

The laws we follow

Nerina follows Italian and European child safety laws. Among them:

  • the Italian Criminal Code on child sexual abuse material, including virtual material, and on the grooming of children (articles 600-ter, 600-quater, 600-quater.1, 609-undecies);
  • Italian laws 269/1998 and 38/2006 against the sexual exploitation of children, including online;
  • Directive 2011/93/EU on combating the sexual abuse and sexual exploitation of children;
  • the Digital Services Act (Regulation (EU) 2022/2065), including the protection of minors online;
  • the General Data Protection Regulation (Regulation (EU) 2016/679).

Who to contact

The child safety point of contact is Nerina's child safety officer. Email: support@nerina.eu
