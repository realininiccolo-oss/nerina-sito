Invite-only pilot · from 16, with a verified ID · European Union

1

Why Nerina

Social networks have turned into media.

An endless stream to watch, designed to keep you there. But what you are really looking for is people like you, who speak your language.

Nerina starts again from there: nothing to scroll, nothing to post.

2

How it works

You tell. The sparrow searches.

  1. In the Diary you say, in writing or out loud, what you need: someone to run with, a job close to home, a place to listen to jazz.
  2. The sparrow searches among the people and businesses on Nerina, using only what each person has chosen to make available.
  3. A leaf arrives: a proposal. You see each other under a pseudonym and reveal yourselves only if you both want to.
3

The sparrow

It works for you, not for your screen time.

  • It proposes, it does not decide. You accept or decline every leaf.
  • It knows it is a program. It never pretends to be a person and has no romantic or sexual conversations.
  • It knows when to stop. It has a daily work limit, then it rests until tomorrow.
  • It is European. It uses a model by Mistral AI, in France, and never receives your surname, date of birth or photo.
4

Privacy

By default, nobody sees anything.

You choose, detail by detail, who sees what.

  1. Nobody default
  2. Sparrows
  3. Interested
  4. Connections
  5. Public
  • In a leaf you stay under a pseudonym until you both reveal yourselves.
  • The most sensitive details are saved only with your yes, one by one.
  • Your content is not sold, not used for advertising and not used to train AI.
  • If you delete your account, we erase your data and destroy the key that encrypts it.

What is ready and what is missing: the privacy policy

5

Safety

Real people, clear rules.

You can join from 16, after an identity document is verified, with one account per person. People aged 16 or 17 only get proposals from people close in age.

You can report and block anyone: a person decides every report.
