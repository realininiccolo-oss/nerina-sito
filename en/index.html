What is Nerina

Nerina is an app to meet people, find work and discover places, with the help of the sparrow, an AI. You can join only by invitation. It is for people who live in the European Union. You can join from the age of 16, after an identity document is verified. Nerina is the app by Nerina Devs, the name under which Niccolò Realini publishes the app.
