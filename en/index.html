Why Nerina

Today's social networks are media. Nerina is made for meeting people.

For years we were promised they would bring us together. Then social networks turned into media: an endless stream of content, built to keep us watching for as long as possible. We follow, we like, we scroll.

And the wish that started it all, finding people like us who speak our own language, got lost at the bottom of the feed. Nerina starts again from there.

How it works

Three steps. No scrolling.

  1. 1

    You tell

    In the Diary you write, or speak, what you need: someone to run with on Sundays, a job close to home, a place with live jazz. It is not a post and it never lands on a wall.

  2. 2

    The sparrow searches

    It searches for you among the people and businesses on Nerina. It only uses what each person has made available to the sparrows: what you left at «Nobody» is off limits, even for it.

  3. 3

    A leaf arrives

    A leaf is a proposal: a person, a job, a place or an event. You see each other under a pseudonym; you accept or decline, and you reveal yourselves only if you both choose to.

The sparrow

An AI that works for you, not for your screen time.

The sparrow is the heart of Nerina: it listens, searches and proposes. Then it steps aside, because you always decide.

  • It listens the way you talk

    Write to it as you would to a friend, or talk to it: the audio becomes text and is not stored.

  • It proposes, it does not decide

    You accept or decline every proposal. No solely automated decision with legal effects on you.

  • It knows it is a program

    It never pretends to be a person and it can make mistakes. It has no romantic or sexual conversations, with anyone.

  • It knows when to stop

    It has a daily work limit for each person: when it reaches it, it rests until the next day. No reason to keep you glued to the screen.

  • European, and discreet

    It uses a language model by Mistral AI, a French company, which does not keep your messages after the reply. The sparrow never receives your surname, date of birth or photo.

Privacy

Privacy is not a setting. It is the design.

On social media your profile is a shop window. On Nerina you choose, detail by detail, who sees what, and by default nobody does.

The five visibility levels

  1. NobodydefaultOnly you. Not even the sparrows use it.
  2. SparrowsUsed by the sparrows for matching. No person reads it.
  3. InterestedPeople with a leaf or a match in progress with you.
  4. ConnectionsYour connections and people with a leaf with you.
  5. PublicVerified people on Nerina. Never the web.

  • Pseudonymous until you both say yes

    In a leaf the other person sees you under a pseudonym, until you both choose to reveal yourselves.

  • The Diary is not written to disk

    The day's text stays only in memory until «Close the day». Then a summary remains, without special categories of data.

  • Sensitive details, only with a yes

    Political opinions, religion, sexual orientation: saved only with a yes for each one, and they start at «Nobody».

  • A photo to recognise each other, nothing more

    No facial recognition. We strip location and phone model from the file, and the photo never goes to the language model.

  • Businesses only see groups

    Aggregated data of at least 5 people. For jobs, only what your CV shows to businesses.

  • Off the web

    Even «Public» stays inside Nerina: it is not indexed and not exported.

  • Hide from people you know

    You can hide what you share from your connections, or from theirs too.

  • Your content is not for sale

    We do not use it for advertising, we do not sell it and we do not use it to train AI models.

  • Delete, and the key is gone

    When you delete your account we erase your data and destroy the key that encrypts it. From the web too, without the app.

Nerina is in its pilot: what is ready and what is still missing is written in the privacy policy.

Safety

Real people, clear rules.

Read our child safety standards

  • Verified identity

    You can join from the age of 16, after an identity document is verified. One account per person.

  • Extra protection at 16 and 17

    Proposals only with people close in age, and no partner search.

  • Report, block, a person decides

    You can report and block anyone. A person decides every report and explains the decision.

  • Meetings in public places

    Proposals to meet point to a public place.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost?

During the pilot Nerina is free: you pay nothing and there are no ads.

How do I join?

During the pilot you can join only with a personal invitation. You must be at least 16 and verify an identity document.

Does the sparrow read what I write?

Yes, because it needs to understand what you are looking for. The text goes to Mistral AI's language model (France), which does not keep it after the reply. The sparrow never receives your surname, date of birth or profile photo.

Who sees my profile?

Whoever you choose, detail by detail, with five levels: from «Nobody» to «Public». By default every detail is at «Nobody». Even «Public» stays inside Nerina.

Where is my data?

The language model comes from a European provider, Mistral AI. For the pilot, hosting is planned in a data centre in the European Union. Details are in the privacy policy.

Can I delete everything?

Yes, whenever you want: in the app, or on the web without the app. We erase your data and destroy the key that encrypts it. Delete your account.
