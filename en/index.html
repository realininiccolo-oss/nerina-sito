What is Nerina

Nerina is an app to meet people, find work and discover places, with the help of the sparrow, an AI.

  • You can join only by invitation.

  • It is for people who live in the European Union.

  • You can join from the age of 16, after an identity document is verified.

Nerina is published under the name Nerina Devs. The data controller is named in the privacy policy.
