One person, several roles

You are many people. All of them real.

With colleagues, with your running partner, with people who share a passion: each context needs different details. On Nerina you have up to three usernames, one per social role, each with its own avatar and only the details you choose. People you meet see the username that fits that context.

Behind every username there is an identity verified with a document. Be whoever you want, but always yourself.

The sparrow

The sparrow connects. You decide.

You tell the Diary what you are looking for. The sparrow, an AI, searches among Nerina's verified people and brings you a leaf: a proposal that has to make sense for both of you. No scores, no feed: you accept or decline.

YouI'm looking for someone to run with on Sunday mornings.

Someone elseI'd like a running partner close to home.

Leaf Running together · Sunday morning · in a public park

YouI need help building the website for my project.

Someone elseI'd like to work on a new project in the evenings.

Leaf Work and business · a shared project

YouI'm organising a card tournament at the parish hall.

Someone elseI'd love to play cards with new people.

Leaf Event · card tournament · Saturday

YouOur bookshop is hosting a meet-the-author evening.

Someone elseI'm looking for book talks near home.

Leaf Event · meet the author · only if you allowed it

European privacy

Born in Europe. Closed by default.

Nerina is built around European social culture: European privacy rules are the starting point, not an obstacle. Every detail starts at «Nobody» and you open it, one level at a time. So what counts is what you share, not how exposed you are.

  • The sparrow uses a European model by Mistral AI (France) and never receives your surname, date of birth or photo.
  • Your content is not sold, not used for advertising and not used to train AI.
  • If you delete your account we erase your data and destroy the key that encrypts it.

Safety

Real people, clear rules.

You can join from 16, after an identity document is verified, with one account per person. People aged 16 or 17 only get proposals from people close in age. You can report and block anyone: a person decides every report.

Our child safety standards
