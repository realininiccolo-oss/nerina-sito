Which data we collect

For each item: what, why, and the legal basis (GDPR Article 6 and, for special categories, Article 9).

  • Identity: first name, surname, date of birth. Needed for the minimum age of 16, for the rules for 16-17 year olds and to have one account per person. You cannot change them yourself: you correct them with a rectification request. Basis: contract (Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • Profile photo: a photo of your face, required. See The profile photo.
  • Your Window: a random string of letters and numbers you share (copy, QR or link) so that someone can ask to connect with you. It does not contain your name. Basis: contract.
  • Profile, Topics and CV: what you write or choose (town, interests, availability, languages, experience…), each with the visibility level you choose. Basis: contract; for levels above «Nessuno» (Nobody), your choice.
  • Diary: what you write to the sparrow, also by voice. The day's text stays only in memory until «Chiudi giornata» (Close the day): it is not written to disk. What remains is the day card (a summary without special-category data) and the notes. Basis: explicit consent to the diary (Art. 9(2)(a)), separate and revocable, because in the diary you may write sensitive things.
  • Special-category data (political opinions, religion, sexual orientation, appearance and aesthetic preferences): saved only with a yes for each item (Art. 9(2)(a)); they start at «Nessuno» (Nobody) and are used to look for people only if you raise them at least to «Passeri» (Sparrows). An adult can also make them visible to people (up to «Pubblica», Public); appearance and aesthetic preferences stay at most at «Passeri». Health, sex life, ethnic origin, genetic and biometric data, trade union membership, criminal offences: never saved.
  • Leaves, connections, areas, personal names: to suggest meetings, places, products and work. Basis: contract.
  • Events: the events you organise, those you attend, and who sees that you attend (nobody at the start). Basis: contract.
  • Wind: if you turn it on, your tastes per department count, anonymously, in the tastes of large groups. Off at the start, chosen department by department. Basis: consent (Art. 6(1)(a)).
  • Targeted offers: if you turn them on for a department, a subscribed company can offer you one of its products. Off at the start. Basis: consent (Art. 6(1)(a)).
  • Ratings, declared purchases, reviews: optional. Basis: contract; reviews are public under a pseudonym.
  • Messages to organisations: they arrive without your name. In our systems the sender stays saved, for limits and security. Basis: contract.
  • Reports: who reports, what, the reason; the decisions and their reasons. Basis: legal obligation (DSA Articles 16-17).
  • Consent log: what you accepted or withdrew and when, with a fingerprint of the text (never the text). Basis: obligation to demonstrate consent (Art. 7(1)).
  • The name you give the sparrow: only you see it. It does not go to the language model.
  • The sparrow's counter: how much it worked for you today, for the daily limit.
  • Notifications: today only inside the app (the bell). If phone notifications arrive in the pilot, they will have a generic text, without names, photos or what you wrote.

Who sees your data

Each profile item has one of 5 levels. You choose it and change it whenever you want.

  • Nessuno (Nobody): only you. Not even the sparrows use it.
  • Passeri (Sparrows): only the sparrows, for matching. No person reads it.
  • Interessati (Interested): the people you have a leaf or a match in progress with, and the sparrows.
  • Connessioni (Connections): your connections, whoever has a leaf with you, and the sparrows.
  • Pubblica (Public): all verified people on Nerina, and the sparrows. Not the web: it is not indexed and not exported.

At the start every item is at «Nessuno». To raise a level you need identity verification; lowering it is always free.

  • Escludi (Exclude): you can exclude people or areas from an item. Blocking wins over everything.
  • Escludi conoscenti (Exclude acquaintances): you can exclude your connections, or also their connections, from what you share (profile data, CV, Wind, network, name). It only applies to people connected to you on Nerina. Someone who knows you but is not your connection here is not excluded.
  • Companies and venues see only aggregates of at least 5 people and, for work, what your CV shows to «Aziende (HR)» (never your ideal salary as a number).
  • In leaves the other person sees you under a pseudonym, until you both choose to reveal yourselves.

The profile photo

  • What: a photo of your face, frontal and close up. It is required to have an account. It is the only photo Nerina collects (for an organisation, the logo): no photos are uploaded in the Diary, leaves, products or events.
  • Why: so that people can recognise each other when they meet in person. Basis: contract (Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • Who sees it: whoever you choose, with the same 5 levels («Connessioni» at the start); you can make it visible to nobody. For the photo «Passeri» counts as «Nessuno». «Escludi» and «Escludi conoscenti» also apply to the photo. Before you and the other person reveal yourselves, and in anonymous connections, the photo is never shown. An association sees its members' photos, if they are visible at least to connections. For 16-17 year olds the highest level is «Connessioni».
  • Who does not see it: the sparrows, the language model and companies (also for work). The photo does not enter searches, matching, the Wind or summaries, and it does not go to the model provider.
  • No facial recognition: on upload there is only the automatic check that there is a face in the photo, one face and close up, done in our systems. It does not recognise who you are, does not compare the photo with other photos or documents, and does not save face measurements. In the pilot a person also looks at the photo, together with identity verification.
  • The file: we re-encode it and remove hidden data (for example location and phone model). It is shown only inside Nerina, never at a public address.
  • How long: as long as the account exists. When you change it, the old one is deleted at once; when you delete the account, it is deleted with the rest.

The sparrow and the language model

The sparrow is an artificial intelligence, not a person. To answer you and search for you it uses a language model from an external provider: Mistral AI (France, European Union), which processes the data on Nerina's behalf (Art. 28).

  • What the model receives: the text of the day's diary, your first name, your most recent notes and preferences, the texts of your Topics needed for the answer (including special-category data you saved), and the first names of your connections that you already see.
  • What it does not receive: surname, date of birth, the profile photo, the name you give the sparrow.
  • Voice: if you dictate to the sparrow, the audio goes to the provider (Mistral AI, transcription service) and comes back as text. The audio is not saved. At most 60 seconds at a time.
  • A daily limit: the sparrow has a work limit for each person and each day. When it reaches it, it rests until the next day. The answer in moments of crisis has no limit: it is a fixed text with useful numbers.
  • No solely automated decision with legal effects (Art. 22): for work the comparison is made by a hand-written rule, and the company decides. See How we choose.

Who receives the data

  • Model and voice provider: Mistral AI (France). The processing agreement (Art. 28) is not signed yet.
  • Hosting: planned for the pilot, Scaleway, in a data centre in the European Union. Today the test version runs on a computer of the operator.
  • Identity verification: in the pilot an external provider, still to be chosen. Today it is simulated.
  • Other people: they see only what you made visible, and the leaves that concern you (under a pseudonym).
  • Companies and venues: only aggregates of at least 5 people, the Wind of large groups (never people under 18) and, for work, what the CV shows to «Aziende (HR)».
  • Outside the European Union: we do not plan transfers. We are checking it with the providers and their sub-providers.

How long we keep it

  • Diary text: until «Chiudi giornata», only in memory.
  • Memory notes: 12 months.
  • Short chat: 24 hours, only in memory. A reported message stays until the decision plus 30 days.
  • Special-category proposals without an answer: until the end of the day, then they disappear without a trace.
  • «Solo se è reciproco» (Only if mutual) consents: 30 days.
  • Voice audio: not saved.
  • Profile photo: as long as the account exists; the old one is deleted when you change it.
  • Profile, Topics, CV, leaves, connections, areas, events: as long as the account exists.
  • Reports, consent log, access log of the Nerina team: the period is still to be decided.
  • When you delete the account your data is deleted. What remains: other people's leaves with you, with the words «Persona non più su Nerina» (Person no longer on Nerina); reports, without your name; a line saying an account was deleted, with a fingerprint instead of your id. Today, in the test version, other data also remains (events you organised and registrations, Wind and offer consents, «Escludi conoscenti», the sparrow's counter): we will delete it before the pilot.
  • In the pilot, after deletion, a fingerprint of your identity will remain for 30 days, so that nobody opens a second account with the same identity right away.

Your rights and how to use them

They are free. Where there is no path in the app, write to the email or PEC on Contacts: the answer arrives within one month, as the GDPR requires (Art. 12).

  • Access (Art. 15): «Profilo › Dati e account › Esporta» gives you the file of your data. The consents you gave, with the date, are in «Profilo › Privacy e consensi». Today the file does not yet contain events and registrations, Wind and offer consents, «Escludi conoscenti», rectification requests, sent connection requests and the sparrow's name: ask for them by email.
  • Rectification (Art. 16): you change Profile, Topics and CV yourself. First name, surname and date of birth: «Profilo › Identità › Segnala un errore»; a person reviews it. CV experiences cannot be deleted: you change their visibility or ask for a rectification.
  • Erasure (Art. 17): «Profilo › Dati e account › Elimina» (type ELIMINA to confirm). A single item: «Elimina» on the item in Topics; a memory: «Dimentica» in «Diario › Gestione».
  • Restriction (Art. 18): by email or PEC.
  • Notification to recipients (Art. 19): by email or PEC.
  • Portability (Art. 20): the same Export file, in JSON format.
  • Objection (Art. 21) and withdrawal of consent (Art. 7(3)): in «Profilo › Privacy e consensi» turn off the Diary, the Wind, targeted offers, «Proponi locali iscritti» and what the sparrow says only if mutual; in Topics set an item back to «Nessuno». Withdrawal applies at once for the future (for the Wind, from the next week). For the rest, by email or PEC.
  • Automated decisions (Art. 22): no solely automated decision with legal effects on you. To contest a proposal or a decision, by email or PEC.
  • Complaint: you can lodge a complaint with the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali (www.garanteprivacy.it) or with the authority of your EU country.

If you are 16 or 17

  • Nerina is for people from 16. Under 18 your identity is always verified.
  • No partner search; suggestions with other people only if your dates of birth are at most 3 years apart.
  • Special-category data stays at most at «Passeri»; the profile photo at most at «Connessioni».
  • Your contribution to the Wind counts only in the people's Wind: companies do not see it.
  • Work only in job ads open to 16-17 year olds; no products for adults only.

What is not ready yet

Today Nerina is a test version: the data is in an unencrypted file (with synthetic people), access is simplified, identity verification is simulated and whoever runs the test can read everything. The pilot with real people does not start until these things are ready.
