Contact

Nerina is the app by Nerina Devs. Nerina Devs is the name under which Niccolò Realini publishes the app. During the pilot it is run by this person, not a company, based in Erbusco (BS), Italy.

Write to us

  • Email: support@nerina.eu
  • Certified email (PEC, Italy): nerinanetwork@pec.it

We have no phone number. We reply in Italian and in English, within [?]. If you saw an error code in the app, for example E-7F3A, include it in your message. We never ask for your password or for the 6-digit code.

What for

  • Help with the app: support@nerina.eu.
  • Privacy and your rights: support@nerina.eu or the PEC. We reply within one month. Read the privacy policy.
  • Deleting your account: go to Delete your account. You do not need the app.
  • Reporting content or a person: in the app, tap Report. If you have no account, write to support@nerina.eu and tell us what you saw and where.
  • Child safety: the point of contact is Niccolò, at support@nerina.eu. Read the standards.
  • Authorities: support@nerina.eu or the PEC, in Italian or English.

In an emergency

Nerina is not an emergency service. If someone is in danger, call 112 (in Italy and the EU).

Privacy complaints

You can contact the Italian data protection authority, Garante per la protezione dei dati personali: www.garanteprivacy.it.
