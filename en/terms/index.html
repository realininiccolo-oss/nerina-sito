Terms of Use (courtesy translation)

Draft of 10 November 2026 (v1) · Invite-only pilot · Italiano

This English text is a courtesy translation. If the two versions differ, the Italian version prevails.

These are the rules for using Nerina during the pilot. You accept them when you sign up, before you confirm your email and verify your identity. If you do not agree, do not use Nerina: you can delete your account at any time.

In short

  • Nerina is a free, invite-only pilot run by one person, not by a company.
  • You must be at least 16 and verify your identity. One account per person.
  • If you are 16 or 17, extra rules apply to protect you.
  • The sparrow is an AI: it can make mistakes. You always decide.
  • No hate, harassment, sexual content, violence, scams, spam or false identities. Zero tolerance for anything that exploits or endangers people under 18.
  • You can report and block. A person decides every report, we explain the decision, and you can complain.
  • You can delete your account at any time, in the app or on the web.

1. Who provides Nerina

  • Nerina is provided by Niccolò Realini, a natural person, based in Erbusco (BS), Italy. It is not a company and has no VAT number.
  • Email: support@nerina.eu · Certified email (PEC): nerinanetwork@pec.it
  • On this page «we» and «the operator» mean this person. «You» are the person using Nerina.

2. What Nerina and the pilot are

  • On Nerina you write in private what you are looking for. The sparrow, an AI, looks for it among registered people and businesses and brings you proposals («leaves»). You accept or refuse them.
  • The pilot is a trial with few people: you join only with a personal invite; it is free, with no payments and no ads; it is a trial version that can have errors, change, pause or end (see sections 14 and 16).
  • How we use your data is explained in the Privacy policy. It is information we owe you, not an agreement to accept.

3. Who can use Nerina

  • Age: you must be at least 16.
  • Verification: before you can meet other people you verify your identity with an identity card, a passport or a driving licence from a European Union country, on the page of our provider Didit. Your date of birth comes from there: declaring it is not enough.
  • Before verification your account is «pending»: nobody sees you and you receive no proposals or requests.
  • If verification fails the account stays frozen and is deleted automatically after 30 days; we email you 7 days before.
  • If you turn out to be under 16 you cannot use Nerina: we delete the account at once. Only one line without your name stays, for 3 months.
  • One person, one account. You cannot open a second account, including after a suspension or closure decided by us.
  • If a court or an authority has forbidden you from having contact with minors, you cannot use Nerina.

4. Your account

  • Give true data: first name, surname and date of birth must be yours. Your profile photo must show your face.
  • You can have 1 to 3 usernames. Choose them without insults, without other people's names and without pretending to be someone else.
  • Keep your password secret and do not let others use your account. Do not transfer or sell accounts, invites, usernames or Window codes.
  • If you think someone has accessed your account, change your password and write to support@nerina.eu.
  • Give your username and Window only to people you want to be able to send you a connection request.

5. If you are 16 or 17

Our systems apply these rules: they do not depend on the app you use and cannot be turned off.

  • Age difference: we suggest people to you only if your dates of birth are at most 3 years apart.
  • Adults: people over 20 cannot reach you with an invite or a targeted proposal.
  • Connection requests: nobody can send you a photo in a request, and you cannot send one. People over 20 can only write you a short text message, which you can report.
  • Looking for a partner (dating): in the App Store app (iPhone) it does not exist for anyone. In the Google Play app it exists only from the age of 18: it is not available to people aged 16 or 17. Where looking for a partner is available, people aged 16 or 17 are only matched with people at most 3 years apart.
  • Sensitive data: your special-category data (for example health, religion, orientation) stays at most at the «Sparrows» level; your photo at most at «Connections».
  • Not available to you: «What the other feels», «Targeted leaf», [da completare]
  • Work: only job ads open to people aged 16 or 17.
  • Reports that concern you are handled first.
  • If someone makes you uncomfortable, or asks you for photos, money or to move to another app: block, report and talk to an adult you trust. If you are in danger call 112. In Italy you can also call Telefono Azzurro on 19696 or 114 (Children's Emergency).
  • Anyone aged 18 or over who uses Nerina must respect these rules towards you (see section 8).

6. The sparrow is an artificial intelligence

  • The sparrow is not a person. It is software that uses a language model by Mistral AI. It receives what you write only after your consent (see the Privacy policy).
  • It can make mistakes: it can misunderstand, forget, suggest something irrelevant or say things that are not true. Always check important information.
  • It is not a doctor, a psychologist, a lawyer or an adviser. Do not use its answers in place of a professional.
  • Its proposals are proposals, not decisions. You accept or refuse. Nerina decides nothing for you.
  • It does not vouch for people: when it suggests someone, it is not telling you that person is trustworthy.
  • The sparrow does not hold romantic or sexual conversations.
  • If you write that you are in danger, it replies with a fixed text listing useful numbers (112, 1522). It does not replace emergency services: in an emergency call 112.
  • If an answer from the sparrow is wrong, offensive or worrying, report it: tap the answer and choose «Report».
  • Do not try to make it do what these terms forbid, and do not pass off its answers as a person's words.

7. Meeting in person

  • Leaves between people who do not know each other suggest a public place. Stay there, at least the first time.
  • Nerina verifies a document, not intentions. We do not check criminal records and cannot guarantee how a person will behave.
  • Tell someone where you are going. Do not give money, passwords or documents. If something feels wrong, leave, block and report.
  • You decide whether and how to meet someone.

8. What is forbidden

This applies to everything you write, upload or do on Nerina: profile, usernames, photos, messages, requests, events, reviews, ads, and what you ask the sparrow to look for or say.

Never, for any reason: exploitation of, and danger to, people under 18

  • Sexual content involving minors, real or invented, including asking for it or offering it.
  • Grooming: gaining a minor's trust in order to obtain sexual contact or intimate images.
  • Blackmail using intimate images, real or alleged.
  • Any form of exploitation or trafficking.
  • If you are 18 or over: asking a person aged 16 or 17 for images, their address, their school or other personal data; offering them gifts or money; asking them to keep the contact secret.
  • If you are 18 or over: using Nerina to get a person aged 16 or 17 whom you do not already know in person to talk to you outside the app (other chats, phone, social media) or to meet you in a non-public place.
  • Trying to get round the rules in section 5, for example by lying about your age or using someone else's account.

In these cases we close the account immediately. We inform the authorities when a crime is suspected (see section 11).

Hate

  • Inciting hatred, violence or discrimination against people or groups because of origin, skin colour, religion, disability, age, nationality, sexual orientation, sex, gender identity, immigration status or similar characteristics.
  • Symbols, slogans or references to hate groups.

Harassment and threats

  • Threatening, insulting, humiliating, stalking, blackmailing.
  • Insisting after a no, a refusal or a block; looking for a person with another username after they blocked you.
  • Publishing or spreading other people's private data (address, phone, photos, conversations) without their consent.

Sexual content

  • Pornography, nudity, sexual messages or images, including between adults.
  • Offering or asking for sex or company in exchange for money, gifts or favours.
  • Intimate images of other people, real or created with AI.

Violence and danger

  • Threats or glorification of violence, terrorism and violent extremism.
  • Content that encourages self-harm, suicide or eating disorders.
  • Selling or seeking weapons, drugs or other illegal goods; promoting alcohol or tobacco to people under 18.

Scams and deception

  • Asking for money, loans, investments, cryptocurrency, bank details or codes.
  • Fake or pay-to-apply job offers, chain letters, pyramid schemes.
  • Fake or paid reviews.

Spam

  • Unsolicited advertising, repeated or bulk messages, mass invitations.
  • Using automated programs to create accounts, send requests or collect other people's data.
  • Driving people to other services for commercial purposes.

False identities

  • Pretending to be another person, a business or an authority; using someone else's document, name or photo.
  • Declaring an age, title or role you do not have.
  • Opening more than one account or coming back after a closure.

Other forbidden things

  • Anything illegal in Italy, including infringing other people's copyright or trade marks.
  • Putting other people in photos or messages without their knowledge.
  • Attacking or probing Nerina's security, trying to read other people's data, getting round limits and blocks.
  • Making reports or complaints you know to be false in order to harm someone.

9. Your content

  • What you write and upload remains yours.
  • You give us only the permission needed to run Nerina: storing your content, processing it with the sparrow and showing it to the people you chose through the visibility levels. The permission is free, non-exclusive and ends when you delete the content or the account (except for what the Privacy policy says is kept for a limited time).
  • We do not use your content for advertising, do not sell it and do not use it to train AI models.
  • Upload only things you have the right to use. You are responsible for what you publish.

10. Reporting and blocking

Reporting

  • In the app: «Report» on [da completare]. You do not need to leave the app.
  • Without an account, or if you prefer: write to support@nerina.eu.
  • To help us decide, tell us: what you are reporting and where it is; why you think it is illegal or against these terms; your name and email (not needed if you report child sexual abuse); that what you write is true to the best of your knowledge.
  • What happens next: we confirm we received the report; a person, not a program, examines it; reports about safety or a minor come first, and we aim to decide them within 24 hours, the others within 3 days; we tell you what we decided and how you can challenge it.
  • The reported person is not told who reported them, unless it is strictly necessary or required by law.

Blocking

  • You can block anyone. The block applies to all usernames of that person; the connection and the chat end.
  • You can unblock in Profile › Privacy and consents › Blocked people. Unblocking does not restore the connection.

11. How we moderate

  • Automated checks, with no person reading: when you upload a profile photo, a check only looks for one face in close-up; a list of forbidden words for usernames; limits on attempts and requests; the age and dating rules, applied by our systems; the sparrow, which refuses forbidden requests and replies with a fixed text in a crisis.
  • Human review: the operator examines every report and every complaint. During the pilot the operator also looks at profile photos. No account suspension or closure is decided by a program.
  • The operator does not read your diary, unless a law or an authority requires it. If you report an answer from the sparrow, the operator reads that answer only.
  • We apply the rules carefully, in the same way for everyone and in proportion to seriousness, taking your rights into account, including freedom of expression.
  • Serious crimes: if we become aware of something suggesting a crime that threatens a person's life or safety, we inform the authorities immediately. If we find child sexual abuse material we remove it, keep what investigators need and report it to the Italian Postal Police.
  • The rules that protect people under 18 are also in Child safety.

12. What we can decide and how we explain it

If you break these terms or the law, depending on seriousness and on whether it is the first time, we can: (1) warn you; (2) remove or hide content; (3) limit a feature for a period; (4) suspend the account, for at most 30 days; (5) close the account. For the most serious facts (minors, threats, scams, false identities, sexual content) we go straight to suspension or closure.

Whenever we take one of these decisions we send you, at the same time, a written statement of reasons, in the app and by email, with:

  • what we decided, what it covers and how long it lasts;
  • the facts we rely on, and whether we started from a report or from our own check;
  • whether we used automated tools to find the content or to decide;
  • which law we believe was broken and why, or which section of these terms and why;
  • what you can do: complaint (section 13) and court (section 17).

13. Complaints

  • You can challenge our decision within 6 months from when we told you. This applies if the decision affects you and also if you made the report and disagree with the outcome.
  • How: from the message with the statement of reasons, or by writing to support@nerina.eu. It is free.
  • A person examines the complaint. We reply within 15 days, explaining why.
  • If you are right, we reverse the decision without delay: we restore the content or reopen the account.
  • During the pilot Nerina is run by one person: the person who examines the complaint is the same person who took the decision. We tell you this plainly.
  • You always keep the right to go to court and, for breaches of the EU Digital Services Act, to AGCOM, the competent authority in Italy.

14. Suspension, closure and end of the pilot

  • For breaches: see sections 12 and 13.
  • For security: we can briefly freeze an account if someone else appears to be using it. We tell you immediately and reopen it as soon as the matter is cleared up.
  • End or pause of the pilot: we can close or pause the pilot. We email you at least [da completare] before, unless we have to stop immediately for security or legal reasons. Before closure you can download your data (Profile › Data and account › Export).
  • When an account is closed, data is deleted as described in the Privacy policy.

15. Deleting your account

  • You can leave whenever you want, without giving a reason and at no cost.
  • In the app: Profile › Data and account › Delete.
  • On the web, even without the app: nerina.eu/en/delete-account.
  • We delete your data and ask the verification provider to delete theirs. What remains for a limited time, without your name, is described in the Privacy policy.
  • Before deleting you can download a copy of your data.

  • The sparrow may stop if the service exceeds its spending cap or if we must stop it for safety. The useful numbers always stay active.
  • We can add, change or remove features. If a change takes away something important, we tell you first (see section 18).
  • What other people and businesses write and do is their responsibility. We are liable where the law says so, for example if we know about illegal content and do nothing.
  • We are not liable for damage caused by events outside our control, such as a failure of the network or of your phone.
  • Nothing in these terms excludes or limits: our liability for wilful misconduct or gross negligence; our liability for death or personal injury; the rights that the Italian Consumer Code and other laws give you and that you cannot waive.
  • If one rule in these terms turns out to be invalid, the others remain valid. In case of doubt, the reading most favourable to you applies.

17. Law and courts

  • Italian law applies to these terms.
  • If you use Nerina as a consumer, the competent court for any dispute is the court of the place where you live or are domiciled.
  • If you live in another EU country you keep the protection of the mandatory rules of your country.
  • Before going to court you can write to us: we will try to solve it together.

18. Changes to these terms

  • We tell you in the app and by email at least [da completare] before, showing what changes. Urgent changes required by law or for safety can apply immediately.
  • If you do not agree you can delete your account before the new terms take effect, at no cost. For important changes we ask you to accept them again.
  • Each version has a date. Earlier versions are at [da completare].

  • Whoever registers a business or organisation declares that they can represent it and accepts the business verification.
  • Sections 3-18 also apply to businesses. The consumer protections in sections 16 and 17 do not apply to businesses.
  • A business's ads, proposals and events must be true, lawful and recognisable as its own. Job ads state the pay. A business must not try to identify the people behind pseudonyms or use data for purposes other than the proposal.
