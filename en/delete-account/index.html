Delete your Nerina account

Nerina is the app by Nerina Devs. On Google Play it is listed as "Nerina: people, work, places". Nerina is published under the name Nerina Devs. The data controller is named in the privacy policy. See Contact. This page explains how to delete your account and your data. You can do it without the app.

On the web, without the app

You do not need the app on your phone and you do not need to download it again.

  1. Enter the email of your account below.
  2. We send a 6-digit code to that email. It is valid for 15 minutes.
  3. Enter the code and confirm.

The form is not on this page: use the way by email below.

By email

If the form does not work, write to support@nerina.eu from the address of your account. Use the subject "Delete account". We ask you to confirm at that address, then we delete. We reply within one month.

In the app

  1. Open Profile › Data and account › Delete.
  2. Enter your password.
  3. Confirm.

What happens

When you confirm, we delete your data and destroy the key that encrypts it. After that there is no way back. It is not a suspension. Timing: from the form and from the app [?]. By email, within one month.

What we delete

  • Email, password and sessions.
  • First name, last name, date of birth and the result of your verification.
  • Usernames and Window codes.
  • Your profile photo.
  • Profile, topics, special-category data and CV.
  • Your diary cards and the sparrow's memory notes.
  • Connections, requests, hints you sent and received, blocks, areas and personal names.
  • Wishes, purchases, likes and your contribution to Wind.

Mistral, which runs the sparrow, does not keep your messages after it answers. We ask Didit, which verified your identity, to delete your verification: see the table below.

Deleting only some data

You do not have to delete your account to delete one piece of data. In the app you can use "Delete" on an item, "Stop recording" on a topic and "Remove consent" on special-category data. See Your rights.

Accounts that are deleted automatically

  • A sign-up with an email that was never confirmed is deleted after 7 days.
  • A sign-up that Nerina did not confirm is deleted after 30 days. We email you 7 days before. If you appeal, we wait for our answer first.
  • If the verification shows you are under 16, we delete the account at once. Only one line without your name stays, for 3 months.

Questions

Write to support@nerina.eu. Details are in the privacy policy.
